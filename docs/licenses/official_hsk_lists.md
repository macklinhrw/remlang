License / reuse terms:

- The Ministry PDF is a stronger official provenance source for the standard and
  associated level lists than third-party mirrors or OCR repositories.
- However, no Creative Commons, BSD, MIT, or similar open redistribution
  license was identified on the Ministry page or the PDF during this attribution
  pass.
- The extracted PDF text also shows repeated `仅供查阅` markings, which suggests
  reference/viewing use rather than an open relicensing grant.
- Separately, the Chinese Test Service legal notice states that website contents
  are copyrighted by `CTI Co., Ltd. (CTI)` or the relevant content providers,
  and that reproduction / quotation should indicate the source as
  `www.chinesetest.cn`.
- Taken together, this means the lists should still be treated as official
  source material with no verified open license, rather than CC-licensed corpus
  data.

Practical attribution note:

- Remlang should attribute these lists to the official Ministry publication as
  the primary provenance source, while treating downstream HSK / CTI pages as
  related official program pages rather than the sole origin.
